🔧 Almost there — connect your Google Sheet

This site needs to know where your sign-in responses live. It takes about five minutes and no coding:

  1. Open your sign-in Google Sheet and share it as “Anyone with the link → Viewer”.
  2. Copy the sheet's web address (URL).
  3. On GitHub, open config.json, click the ✏️ pencil, and paste the URL into "signInDataUrl".
  4. Click Commit changes. The site updates itself in about a minute.

Full walkthrough with pictures of every step: SETUP.md
