🤝 How engineers actually get recruited

1
Join the org for your major
NSBE membership unlocks the national convention & career fair. SWE, SHPE, IEEE, SAE, AIChE, BMES, and ASCE run the same play for their disciplines — companies pay to recruit there.
2
Work the career fairs
Org conventions are massive career fairs with on-site interviews — people walk out with offers. Campus fair weeks (September & January) are the local version.
3
Meet sponsors up close
Companies sponsor chapters to meet you early: info sessions, socials, resume reviews. Sponsor events are warm intros — show up and follow up.
4
Apply inside the window
Most engineering internships open August–November for the following summer. Use the timeline above — applying in week one beats applying in month three.
